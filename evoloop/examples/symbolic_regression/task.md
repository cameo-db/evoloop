# Task: find a formula

## The problem

A dataset has two inputs, `x0` and `x1`, and one output `y`. The output was generated by an unknown
smooth formula of the two inputs plus a little measurement noise. Find a compact functional form
that predicts `y`.

## The data

800 rows: 400 to fit the constants, 200 to score a form, 200 held out. Each input lies between -3
and 3.

## The blocks

- `model` (structure, in `model.py`): defines `N_CONSTANTS` and `predict(x, c)`. `x` is a NumPy array
  of shape `(rows, 2)`; `c` is an array of `N_CONSTANTS` free constants; return an array of
  `rows` predictions. Only NumPy (`np`) is available. The constants are fitted for you: write
  the form, not the numbers.
- `fit_params` (params, in `train.py`): settings of the fitting routine. `lr` is the Adam learning
  rate, `init_scale` the standard deviation of the random initial constants, `l2` the strength of an
  L2 penalty on the constants during fitting, `restarts` the number of random restarts that share the
  step allowance.

## What a good solution looks like

The score is the mean squared error on the scoring rows plus 0.002 per constant, lower is better. A
form with fewer constants wins a tie. Good solutions are short expressions built from elementary
functions.

## Limits

The brief limits the number of constants. A form whose value overflows for inputs in range will
fail to fit.

## What you are shown

For each run: the error on the fitting and the scoring rows, the gap between them, the fitted
constants, and the mean residual and RMSE in three ranges of each input, which shows where a form
is wrong.
